# Knowledge and Information

Knowledge is a first-class simulation system.

## Principle

There is a difference between:

- objective world state
- what a person believes
- what an organization has recorded
- what the player can currently access

These must not be collapsed into one value.

## Acquisition

A person may learn information through:

- direct observation
- participation
- conversation
- rumor
- letters
- books
- accounts
- official records
- inference

## Information metadata

Known information should be able to retain:

- source
- acquisition time
- reliability
- age
- uncertainty

## Local market knowledge

There is no omniscient market-price lookup for ordinary actors.

One merchant may know yesterday's transaction price.

Another may only know a week-old rumor.

A third may know nothing.

## Physical information transfer

Letters and reports move physically through the world.

They may be:

- delayed
- lost
- stolen
- damaged
- intercepted

Orders to distant employees therefore require communication.

## Rumors

Rumors may:

- change through retelling
- lose precision
- accumulate distortions
- be intentionally manipulated

Trust in the source influences how they are interpreted.

## Documents

Documents are concrete objects when appropriate.

Examples:

- correspondence
- contracts
- account books
- deeds
- registers
- wills
- court records

Their informational and evidentiary value may depend on:

- authenticity
- seals
- witnesses
- provenance
- physical survival

## Administration

Administrative UI should derive from actual records.

A business with weak bookkeeping may know only approximate stock.

A business with recent inventory counts and competent clerks may know exact quantities.

## Player-visible information

The normal game UI is a projection of information available to the currently controlled dynasty.

It must not reveal hidden simulation values merely because they are convenient.

Uncertainty may be displayed only when the actor or administration can itself recognize that uncertainty.

## Debug information

Developer tools are explicitly exempt from player knowledge restrictions.

Debug views may inspect:

- true entity state
- hidden relationships
- event chains
- scheduler entries
- objective ownership
- actual disease state

This layer must remain separate from normal gameplay UI.
